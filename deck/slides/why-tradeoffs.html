GPUs, hardware

Power

Tokens become expertise

You trade a per-token bill for the time and skill to set up, secure, and keep patched a system you now own end to end.

Hardware ages fast

Each new model generation wants more VRAM. A GPU that felt generous a year ago can fall behind what the best local models need next.

Scale means more GPUs

One GPU on one desk is fine for one developer. Serving a team means real concurrency; a GPU fleet and the upfront capital cost that comes with it.

For these reasons, every individual and organization will have to find their own sweet spot between local and hosted models.
